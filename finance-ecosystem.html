Finance Ecosystem

Redesigning a bank's digital investing experience from the ground up

BCG Client Project Fintech
Year 2026
Role UX Designer
Ownership Owning the investing stream

(01) — Context

Context

One of Morocco's leading banks wanted to redesign their entire financial ecosystem. The team needed a single design language that could grow with the bank — a complete revamp. I was particularly responsible for one of its newest additions: the investing stream.

(02) — Approach

Approach

Extensive brainstorming with the product team to shape a brand new product. We went through V1, then iterated to V2 using AI to optimize decisions. I collaborated closely with the wider design team, ran testing sessions with engineers, and went through many rounds of client iteration.

(03) — Outcome

Outcome

A cohesive mobile experience aligned to the bank's digital strategy. A complete investing ecosystem designed from scratch — built to scale.

This is a client project. Due to confidentiality, I can only share limited details here. For more information, feel free to reach out: kashishr1603@gmail.com
