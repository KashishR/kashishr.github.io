Parking Pal

A people-friendly bike parking experience at Delft Station

TU Delft 2023 UX Design Physical Interaction

The Challenge

Delft Station bike parking holds 5,000 bicycles. Finding your bike after parking it is stressful and confusing, especially for commuters in a hurry.

Role UX Designer & Researcher
Timeline 2023
Context Master's project, TU Delft

(01) — Research

01

Observations

Studied user behavior patterns in the bike parking facility during peak hours.

02

Shadowing

Followed commuters through their complete parking and retrieval journey.

03

PreMo & Emotion Typology

Mapped emotional responses to different parking scenarios and pain points.

"I want to help people in a hurry at Delft Station Bike Parking to locate their bike effortlessly after parking it."

(02) — Ideation

Rapid Exploration

Through Ultra Rapid Prototypes and Co-creation sessions with commuters, I explored various approaches to help users remember and relocate their parking spots. The iterative process revealed that memory aids combined with physical wayfinding elements showed the most promise.

(03) — Solution

A Two-Part System

Part 1

While Parking

A sentence lights up when the user parks — they read and remember it as their location marker.

Part 2

While Finding

Words on the ground guide users back to their original sentence through intuitive wayfinding.

(04) — Evaluation

Testing Goals

  • Measure the effectiveness of sentence-based memory aids in a high-stress parking environment
  • Evaluate the intuitiveness of ground-level wayfinding elements for bike retrieval
  • Assess user satisfaction and reduced frustration compared to the existing system

(05) — Final Prototype

Bringing the Concept to Life

The final prototype consisted of three components: a detailed 2D layout showing the complete user flow and interaction points, a 3D printed miniature model demonstrating the physical installation in the parking facility, and a working prototype with functional lighting and display elements for user testing validation.
