Retirement Investing

Making complicated retirement investments easy to use for the clients of a major US bank

BCG Client Project Fintech
Year 2025–2026
Role UX Designer
Ownership End-to-end retirement investment experience

(01) — Context

Context

A large US bank wanted to make its complicated retirement investment products easy to use for clients. As a traditional institution accelerating its digitization, they wanted to draw inspiration from modern apps while keeping their own essence and USP intact — and to ship it consistently across phone, tablet, and desktop.

(02) — Approach

Approach

I worked with a cross-functional team of consultants, client designers, and senior leadership through multiple workshops to align on the vision for the new designs. From there, I worked alongside other designers on the team to design the end-to-end retirement investment experience, with constant checkpoints with the client to push the design system toward a more modern, interactive, and visual outcome.

Designed across Desktop, Web App & Native App

01

Desktop

The full experience, used by clients and advisors working through detailed planning. Built on the client's desktop design system.

02

Web App

A lighter, browser-based experience for clients to check in on their retirement investments anytime. Shares the same desktop design system as above.

03

Native App

The primary way clients check in on the go, from their phone. Built on the client's separate native app design system.

(03) — Outcome

Outcome

A whole end-to-end, modernly designed retirement investment app across all three breakpoints — delivered with documentation and new elements added to the design system, ready to be shipped to the client.
