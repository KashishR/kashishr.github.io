Agent-Driven Dashboard

Bringing AI agents into a healthcare company's operations, without losing the trust of a traditional industry

BCG Client Proposal AI Agents in Healthcare
Year 2026
Role UX Designer
Ownership Mapping agent design & interaction model

(01) — Context

Context

A healthcare company with no prior AI adoption wanted to bring AI agents into how its internal teams worked — giving them a company-wide overview of operations alongside specialized insight into their own field. The challenge was introducing agents into a dashboard while preserving the traditional, trustworthy feel healthcare teams expect.

(02) — Approach

Approach

I mapped the landscape of AI agents — from super abstract to highly anthropomorphic — and plotted their agency against the dashboard experience to figure out how much control and personality each agent should carry. I also brainstormed with AI agents themselves to help design the agents for this platform.

(03) — Outcome

Two concepts, proposed to the client

The exploration landed on two distinct directions for how far to push the agentic model.

Concept 1

Dashboard + AI Market Centre

A familiar dashboard augmented with AI agents and an "AI Market Centre" that guides users through discovering and adopting the right agents for their journey.

Concept 2

Fully agentic platform

No traditional cards, KPIs, or navigation. Users interact with a set of specialized agents — insights, campaigns, pricing, service — each proactively surfacing relevant alerts with simple severity indicators and unread counts.
